*TARS Model Catalog Update*

We are pleased to announce expanded model coverage in TARS. Key updates include:

- *Added:* OpenAI GPT-6.1 Terra costs $1 input and $5 output per million tokens, with a 1,050,000-token context window. DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash on Google Cloud costs $0.30 input and $1.20 output, with a 1,048,576-token context window. DeepInfra adds seven models, including DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 and Step-5-Preview.
- *Retired:* OpenAI shut down 10 older models on October 23, 2026, including GPT-3.5 Turbo, GPT-4, o1, o3-mini, o4-mini, and GPT Image 1. Google Cloud retired Gemini 2.5 Pro, Flash, and Flash-Lite and 16 open models. Gemini 2.5 stays available on the Gemini API. DeepInfra retired gpt-oss-120b-Turbo, Inkling, and two Granite 4.2 models.
- *Deprecated:* DeepInfra MythoMax L2 13B retires October 29, 2026. Use L3 8B Lunaris. Hermes 3 Llama 3.1 405B and 70B retire November 2, 2026. Use DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017. OpenAI GPT Image 1.5, GPT Image 1 Mini, and ChatGPT Image Latest retire December 1, 2026. Use GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst or Flare.
- *Price changes:* The DeepInfra GLM-5.3-Flash promotion ended, and its price doubled to $0.15 input and $0.50 output. DeepInfra MiMo-V2.6-Flash, MiMo-V2.6-Pro, and Hy4 Preview have new promotional prices. Grok 4.7, Grok 4.6, and Grok 4.5 now have Batch prices at 20 percent below standard.
- *Other updates:* xAI now accepts files up to 512 MB. Gemini 4 Argon on Google Cloud now runs in `europe-west2` and `asia-southeast1`.

Please move integrations off retired models, and review deprecated models before their retirement dates. Visit the [TARS model catalog](https://router.tetrate.ai/models/) to explore available models and deprecated/retired dates.
